# Can Tho — Mekong River Journey

## 1. Mission
Transform the environmental/WebGL storytelling layer of the existing Kage-based website into:
**Cần Thơ — Mekong River Journey** (Hành Trình Sông Nước Cần Thơ).

One continuous day-to-night river journey through Cần Thơ and the Mekong Delta:
* Ninh Kiều before dawn
* Leave the riverfront & pier threshold
* Sunrise on the Hậu river
* Cái Răng floating market & Cây Bẹo
* Small canals & Nipa palms (Dừa nước)
* Orchards / Miệt vườn & Riverside Southern house
* Sunset & Return to Ninh Kiều at night

## 2. Core Environmental Elements
* Water terrain: Broad Hậu river, narrow canals, water hyacinth (lục bình), reflections
* Rivercraft: Wooden trading boats, ghe, xuồng, sampan, Cây Bẹo with hanging fruits (pineapple, watermelon, pumpkin)
* Architecture: Bến Ninh Kiều pier, wooden docks, Southern riverside wooden houses, distant Cần Thơ cable-stayed bridge silhouette
* Flora: Dừa nước (nipa palm), coconut palms, banana clusters, orchard fruit trees, water hyacinth
* Atmosphere & Time-of-Day: Pre-dawn mist -> Golden sunrise -> Bright morning -> Warm afternoon -> Fiery sunset -> Ninh Kiều night lights
